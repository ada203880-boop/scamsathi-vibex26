        What this tool can and cannot tell you

        ScamSathi uses deterministic rules to explain patterns in message text, URL structure and decoded QR content. It does not use a black-box fraud score or proprietary bank lookup. The score indicates warning sign density, not confirmed fraud guilt; a low score does not guarantee safety. Message content is not retained or logged.
