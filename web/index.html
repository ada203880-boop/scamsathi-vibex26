    What this tool can and cannot tell you

    ScamSathi uses transparent rules to flag known warning patterns in text and URLs. A high score means multiple patterns matched; it is not a verified fraud finding. A low score does not prove safety. It does not access calls, open links, contact banks, block payments, or report incidents for you.
