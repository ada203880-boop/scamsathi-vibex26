      VOICE SCAM SAFETY

      Audio deepfake analysis is not available

      ScamSathi does not upload or analyze recordings and cannot confirm whether a voice is cloned. If a caller urgently asks for money, hang up and call the person using a number you already trust.

      What this tool can and cannot tell you

      ScamSathi uses rules to flag patterns in message text, URL structure and decoded QR content. It does not use a trained fraud model, threat-intelligence feed or bank lookup. The score is not a probability or confirmed verdict; a low score does not prove safety. It does not listen to calls, open links, block payments or submit reports. Message content is not retained.
