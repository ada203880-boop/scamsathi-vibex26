# Demo recording guide

Use the narration in [presentation/DEMO_SCRIPT.md](presentation/DEMO_SCRIPT.md). It is written for a roughly 2–3 minute walkthrough of implemented features and limitations.

## Prepare

1. Confirm the GitHub Pages workflow succeeded before recording from the public demo. For local mode, run `run.bat` or `./run.sh` and open `http://127.0.0.1:18878`.
2. Close personal notifications and unrelated browser tabs. Use only synthetic messages, names, links, and QR data.
3. Confirm the sample message and QR paste flow work. The camera path requires HTTPS or localhost and permission on the device; no camera permission is needed for the manual QR demo.
4. Set display scaling so the result explanations remain readable. Use captions or narration.

## Record

Show the KYC warning, the UPI safety guidance, and a pasted synthetic UPI URI in the QR scanner. Point out the unverified payee/amount and that no payment begins. Explain the session-only dashboard and that links are never visited. Close by stating that the score is not a probability and that ML/audio detection are unavailable.

Keep the recording between 2 and 3 minutes. Follow the current event form's video format and upload limits. This repository contains the script only; it does not claim a demo video has been recorded.

## Before uploading

- Watch the saved video once to confirm audio, captions, and text are clear.
- Confirm it contains no real account data, messages, phone numbers, or private links.
- Disclose the project's pre-event source history, AI tools, and the MIT-licensed QR scanner accurately.
- Ask the organizer whether the pre-event project is eligible.
